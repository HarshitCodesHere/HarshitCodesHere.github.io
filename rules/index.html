Sample participation rules

These draft rules show the decisions an organizer should make visible before registration: team size, original work, conduct, judging and submission requirements.

The following are proposed content headings, not adopted rules.

Team formation

Publish exact team-size limits, whether teams may cross institutions, and whether team matching will be offered.

Project work

State what work must be created during the event, acceptable tools, attribution requirements and any restrictions on previously built projects.

Conduct and judging

Link a code of conduct and describe judging criteria, submission format, accessibility accommodations and how results will be communicated.

See the participation guide for the information students need before registering.
