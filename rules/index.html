Sample rules and team guide

Participation principles

Organizers should publish eligibility, team size, allowed tools, submission format, judging criteria, code of conduct, accessibility support and escalation contacts before registration.

Illustrative team guidance

Teams should agree on a problem, divide tasks, keep a short progress log, credit external assets and prepare a brief demonstration. These are suggestions, not official Bennett University rules.

Fair and safe participation

Respect other participants, protect personal data, use licensed resources, and disclose outside assistance. A real event’s approved rules take precedence.
