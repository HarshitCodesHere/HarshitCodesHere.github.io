About this proposed club concept

Bennett BuildLab is a fictional student tech club concept made for coursework. It is not an official Bennett University club.

Why this prototype exists

Campus event details often sit across posters, forms, and chat groups. This prototype organizes the questions students may ask in one crawlable, accessible site: what the event is, who may participate, how a sample schedule works, and what visitors need to know.

What is confirmed?

This is only a class prototype. No event date, venue booking, registration form, speakers, sponsors, awards, or results are confirmed. The sample pages must not be treated as an event announcement.
