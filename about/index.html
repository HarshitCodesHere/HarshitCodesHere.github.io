A proposed club concept, not an official society

Bennett BuildLab is a fictional club concept for coursework. It demonstrates how students could find event details in one crawlable website.

Bennett BuildLab is a proposed fictional student tech club concept for an academic SEO project. It is not listed as an official Bennett University society and this prototype is not university-endorsed. The intended audience is Bennett students considering a first or repeat hackathon, plus hypothetical organizers and campus visitors.

Project purpose

Replace scattered poster and messaging-group details with stable pages for schedule, eligibility, venue, registration guidance and archives.

Truth and scope

No event date, booked venue, sponsors, speakers, registration form or results are confirmed. Bennett already has technical communities including Bennett Open Source Society and BU HackerEarth; this concept does not claim to represent or replace them.
