Prepare for a first hackathon

Bring a charged laptop, charger, student identification if required, and an idea of what skills you would like to practice. Confirm requirements with the real organizer.

  1. Check the event’s eligibility and team-size rules.
  2. Choose one skill you want to practice and one small problem to explore.
  3. Bring a charged laptop, charger and any required student identification.
  4. Save the venue map and organizer contact before travelling.
  5. Ask about accessibility, food and device or software requirements ahead of time.
  6. Agree on team communication and project ownership before you start.

This checklist is general guidance. Confirm the actual requirements on an official event website. For this example, see the sample rules and FAQ.
