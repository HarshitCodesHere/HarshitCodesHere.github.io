Who can take part?

The example is designed for university students interested in building a small project with a team. A real organizer must confirm eligibility and capacity.

This page shows the information that should be made explicit. All statements below are planning examples, not binding event rules.

Intended audience

University students curious about collaborative building, including beginners. A real organizer must decide whether participation is campus-only, open to other institutions, or limited by age or capacity.

Team and registration checklist

  • Publish minimum and maximum team size.
  • Explain whether solo participants can join a team at the event.
  • Link to a real, privacy-reviewed registration form.
  • State registration opening and closing dates only after confirmation.

No live registration is available for this prototype. Review the sample rules and first-event checklist.
